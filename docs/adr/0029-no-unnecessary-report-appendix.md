# ADR 0029: Keep the final report to the required sections

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-04

## Decision

The final PDF contains only:

- a compact title page;
- a table of contents;
- Answer Part 1: Git workflow;
- Answer Part 2: tests;
- Answer Part 3: AI use and reflection;
- Answer Part 4: app demo.

Do not add an appendix unless a required piece of evidence genuinely cannot be represented in the four answer parts.

## Consequences

- The report remains concise and easy to grade.
- Evidence must be selected and captioned carefully.
- The LaTeX QA loop must catch content that is too dense or too sparse within the constrained structure.

# ADR 0026: Keep AI-use evidence authentic and concise

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-04

## Decision

Create `docs/lab-01/ai_use.md` with:

- the actual AI coding tool and model used by the student;
- 6–10 selected real prompts;
- a brief reflection on how prompting changed during the work;
- the student's own review and responsibility statement.

Do not invent the student's model, prompts, or reflection. Use clearly marked placeholders until the student supplies the final personal details.

## Consequences

- The required AI-use evidence remains truthful and attributable.
- The document stays concise enough for the single-PDF submission.
- The student remains responsible for reviewing and understanding generated work.
